Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating in EUREKA 2025.

Who can submit a proposal for Eureka?
Eureka is open to all university students, recent graduates, researchers, and professional developers across the globe. Teams can range from 1 to 4 members. Both virtual and in-person attendance tracks are supported.
What is the "Ignite: Discovery" theme criteria?
We reward projects that represent a genuine breakthrough—taking a problem previously in the dark and illuminating an elegant, impactful technological solution.
Is participation free?
Yes! Admitted participants receive free access to the hacking venue, API credits, food & drinks, mentor roundtables, and exclusive Eureka merchandise.
Who owns the Intellectual Property (IP) created?
You own 100% of your work. Neither Eureka nor our sponsors claim ownership over any code or intellectual property built during the event.
